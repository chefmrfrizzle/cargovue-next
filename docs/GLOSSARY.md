# Glossary

**Accepted fact** — a proposed statement accepted or corrected by an authorized human reviewer.

**Accreditation** — formal recognition by an accreditation body for a named organization and scope;
not a general quality adjective.

**Audit event** — an append-only record of who or what performed a material action, when, under which
policy/version, and with what result.

**Evidence locator** — exact position in a source version, such as page and bounding box, spreadsheet
sheet and cell range, or media timestamp range.

**Evidence twin** — a graph of evidence, events, methods, reviews, gaps, and dependencies; not a
physical simulation or independent source of truth.

**Gap** — required evidence or information that is absent, unavailable, stale, or unusable.

**Discrepancy** — two or more records or assertions that conflict or cannot yet be reconciled.

**Method profile** — versioned authorization envelope describing exactly when and how a PCE method may
execute.

**PCE** — Physical Calculation Engine.

**Proposal** — machine-generated structured output with no authority until human disposition.

**Release** — frozen, approved, manifested information made available to named recipients.

**Source record** — logical document or item supplied to a matter.

**Source version** — immutable bytes for one received or derived version of a source record.

**Verified** — the explicitly listed integrity or policy checks passed; not a promise of legal truth,
scientific validity outside scope, conformity, accreditation, or liability.

# Institutional trust-center checklist

## Company and governance

- [ ] Named security, privacy, product, engineering, AI, and methodology owners.
- [ ] Security, privacy, acceptable-use, access-control, retention, incident, and secure-development
      policies approved and reviewed.
- [ ] Risk register with owners, treatment, due dates, and accepted residual risk.
- [ ] Employee/contractor screening, agreements, training, onboarding, and offboarding evidence.

## Architecture and data

- [ ] Current architecture and data-flow diagrams.
- [ ] Data classification, residency, retention, legal hold, and destruction rules.
- [ ] Subprocessor register and change-notification process.
- [ ] Tenant isolation design and test report.
- [ ] Encryption/key-management design and rotation evidence.
- [ ] Backup, restore, RTO, and RPO evidence.

## Product and AI

- [ ] Product authority and prohibited-conclusion policy.
- [ ] Model/prompt register, provider terms, training-data settings, and evaluation report.
- [ ] Human-review, correction, appeal, abstention, monitoring, and rollback procedures.
- [ ] Method-profile governance and PCE refusal evidence.
- [ ] Synthetic-data and closed-file qualification protocols.

## Software and operations

- [ ] SDLC, code review, branch protection, dependency, secrets, and vulnerability-management evidence.
- [ ] SBOM and build/deployment provenance.
- [ ] Logging, monitoring, alerting, escalation, and on-call procedures.
- [ ] Independent penetration-test report and remediation evidence.
- [ ] Incident, tabletop, restore, key-loss, worker-compromise, and tenant-escape exercises.

## Customer-facing package

- [ ] Security overview and shared-responsibility model.
- [ ] DPA, security exhibit, service levels, support/escalation, and breach-notification terms.
- [ ] Sample signed evidence package and offline verification receipt.
- [ ] Current certifications/assessments described precisely, without inherited-cloud claims.
- [ ] Open limitations, roadmap items, and residual risks.

## Accreditation language

CargoVue may record and verify evidence about a laboratory’s ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation or an
inspection body’s ISO/IEC 17020 accreditation and applicable scope. CargoVue must not describe itself,
its software, its calculations, or a customer’s result as accredited unless the exact claim is covered
by a valid accreditation issued to the correct legal entity and scope.
